The text showing up on the screen, the Saqta drone's flying system and its GPS -all coming from matrices, bases, eigenvalues and etc. For someone also struggling with their coursework, this document aims to organize all my notes and interpret everything in simple language. 

1. Vector - an arrow in space with magnitude (length) and direction. It is a set of instructions for an arrow. 
2. Span - I imagine this as a playground I can reach by scaling and adding those vectors together in every possible combination (linear combinations).
3. Basis - a set of vectors that act like a building block for vector space. The two rules are being linearly independent (not parallel) in 2D and can not be created from one another) + must span the entire space.
4. A Transition Matrix is a mathematical coordinate translator that changes your viewpoint (basis) without moving or altering anything in the physical space itself. I imagine this as a FIFA game, where I can change my viewpoint or camera angle. Changing view from 1 - 2 position implies making mathematical transformation, yet keeping the same object. "Tele Broadcast" to "Co-op," the game engine runs a Transition Matrix under the hood.
